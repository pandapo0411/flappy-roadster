FLAPPY ROADSTER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Roadster handles information. The app is developed by Hung Le.

1. Information the app stores

Flappy Roadster does not require an account and the developer does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, payment information, photos, microphone data, or other personal information through the game.

The game stores gameplay preferences and progress on your device only, such as unlocked routes, scores, best times, and recent run history. This information is used to provide game features and is not sent to the developer. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising

The production release uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google and its partners may process device and online identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and ad-interaction data to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to Google's own policies and any consent required by applicable law.

You can read Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's data practices.

3. Sharing achievements

When you choose the in-game Share button, Flappy Roadster prepares a text message containing your route, time, score, and temperature result. The app sends that text only to the app or service you select in Android's share sheet. The selected service's privacy policy applies after you share; Flappy Roadster does not receive the shared post or its recipients.

4. Network and permissions

The app requests network-state and internet access to support advertising. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, messages, files, or precise location.

5. Children's privacy

Flappy Roadster does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the developer, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

6. Data retention and security

The developer does not operate a server for player accounts or gameplay records. Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Google's retention and security practices apply to advertising data that it processes.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact
